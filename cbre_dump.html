The Most Sought-after Office Neighbourhoods in Hyderabad

Let Us Do the Hard Work

Let Us Do the Hard Work

In Hyderabad, the most sought-after office neighbourhoods include:

  1. Hitech City: It is in the western part of Hyderabad, which is home to several IT companies and start-ups. This area is well-connected by road and metro rail.
  2. Jubilee Hills: Located in the central part of Hyderabad, an upscale neighbourhood, which is home to several start-ups and creative agencies as it is well connected by road.
  3. Gachibowli: An IT hub, located in the western part of Hyderabad and is home to several multinational companies, which is well connected by road and metro rail.
  4. Banjara Hills: Another upscale neighbourhood, located in the central Hyderabad that is the hub for several start-ups and renowned corporates with proper road connectivity.
  5. Madhapur: IT hub located in the western part of Hyderabad, which is serving as a home to budding start-ups and IT companies as it is well connected by road and metro.
